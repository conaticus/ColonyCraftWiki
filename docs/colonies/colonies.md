# Colonies

A Colony is a team of players in the server who can expand their ownership of land on the map. Members in the same Colony cannot PVP with each other and colonies can have an infinite number of members.

Colony leaders can expand a colony's land across the map so that players who are not a member of that colony are un-able to claim land in that area. See [Colony Creation Guide](/docs/colonies/create-colony.md) for more info.

:::info Tip
Mobs don't spawn in a Colony's controlled land so you don't have to worry about getting attacked!
:::

## Colony Core

Each colony has its own "Colony Core" that is placed when the Colony is created. This is the heart of a colony and your colony depends on this to remain stable.

A colony core is vulnerable and can be destroyed by raiders. If a colony core is destroyed, your colony is deleted forever and all controlled land is lost. See the [Colony Raids](/docs/raids-colonies.md) guide to learn how to raid a colony core and take down a colony!

## Community Chest

A community chest is a very important part of a colony. An infinite number of community chests can be placed inside a colony's land and can be crafted with the [Community Chest Recipe](/docs/recipes/community-chest.md).

Any player can place a community chest in a colony and any player can use them.

To access a community chest, right click on it and a menu will be presented:

<img src="/img/communitychest.gif" />

### Resources

When a colony is created, it will have an upkeep cost. If this cost is not met then the colony will begin to decay. The more land your colony controls, the higher the resource cost will be.

If a colony begins to decay, it will lose 1 chunk of land for every 5 minutes of decay. If all chunks in the colony are lost due to decay, the colony will be deleted forever.

To maintain a colony's resources, the community chest can be used by any player to donate them. To do this, ensure that you have the relevant resource in your inventory, and click on the resource to donate it to the colony. You can hover over the clock in the top right to see the new decay time:

<img src="/img/communitychestresource.gif" />

### Level Up

Each colony can be levelled up at a cost to unlock more expansion slots (meaning more land can be controlled by the colony). This can be done by clicking on the colony core icon in the community chest:

<img src="/img/levelupcolony.png" />

### Repair Colony Core

The final thing you can do with community chests is repair the colony core. If the colony core has been damaged by raiders, it will remain damaged until it is repaired. This can be done by clicked the repair icon in the community chest:
