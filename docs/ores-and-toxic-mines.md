## Important Notes

In Colony Craft, ores are not distributed like in vanilla Minecraft. Instead, they are spread around the map. Some ores only spawn in hotspots and do not spawn anywhere else, this includes:

- Redstone
- Lapis
- Emerald
- Netherite
- Quartz
- Amethyst Cluster

Ores other than these will spawn normally in all areas of the map.

It's important to check what skills you need (if any) to mine the ore you are looking for. You can check if an ore is possible for you to mine by either reading [Mining Levels](/docs/skills/mining.md#levels) or running `/skill_perks Mining` in-game.

## Hotspot Y Levels

Here is a list of all the Y levels that each ore strictly spawns at in a hotspot, these ores will spawn at normal rates (or not at all) above that Y level:

- <strong>Coal: </strong> Y 0 and Below
- <strong>Iron: </strong> Y 0 and Below
- <strong>Redstone: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Gold: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Lapis: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Emerald: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Quartz: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Amethyst Cluster: </strong> Y -10 and Below
- <strong>Diamond: </strong> Y -25 and Below
- <strong>Ancient Debris: </strong> Y -35 and Below

## Special Ores

Colony Craft spawns various special ores that do not typically generate in the overworld in vanilla Minecraft. These have various uses that you can see in [Recipes](/docs/category/recipes).

#### Limonite Ore

- Minimum Pickaxe: Iron Pickaxe
- Y Level: 62 and Below

#### Acanthite Ore

- Minimum Pickaxe: Iron Pickaxe
- Y Level: 0 and Below

#### Valtronium Ore

- Minimum Pickaxe: Diamond Pickaxe
- Y Level: -30 and Below

## Toxic Mines

Some ore hospots contain toxic mines. A toxic mine is an area underground that is filled with toxic gas, which is not safe to enter without a [Gas Mask](/docs/recipes/gas-mask.md):

<img src="/img/toxicmine.gif" width="60%" />

<br />
<br />

These will not start until Y Level -10 in Toxic Zones.

## Gas Masks

Gas Masks can be crafted so that you can enter Toxic Mines safely without dying from gas poisoning: **[See Recipe Here](/docs/recipes/gas-mask.md)**

<img src="/img/gasmask.gif" width="60%" />

<br />
<br />

:::info
A Gas Mask's filter will become full after **30 minutes** of usage in a toxic area.
:::

Gas Masks have a limited durability as their filters can become full, when the filter is full (durability reaching 0), the gas mask will only be partially effective in toxic areas.
