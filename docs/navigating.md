# Navigating the Map

Colony Craft uses a 12,000 x 6000 map of earth with realistic vegetation & biomes! It can be tricky to find where you are on the map, so we have developed a tool to help you find coordinates. This also allows you to see the locations of [Ore Hotspots](/docs/ores-and-toxic-mines.md)!

## Navigation

:::info Tip
Press 't' to open the chat, before clicking the "Click Here" button.
:::

To find out where you are at any time, you can use the `/map` command, and it will give you a link to the browser to reveal your location:

<img src="/img/mapcommand.gif" />

<br />
<br />

At [Mobility](/docs/skills/mobility) Level 2, you also unlock a special compass so you can navigate to specific co-ordinates on the map <strong>([See Guide Here](/docs/Special%20Items/compass))</strong>
