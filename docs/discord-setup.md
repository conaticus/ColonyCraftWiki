# Link Discord Account

Colony Craft has strong integration with our Discord Server, and can do things such as:

- Create colony channels and roles when a colony is created
- Notifications for colony updates e.g the colony decaying

:::info
You must be a member of the Discord Server to link your account. You can join by [<u>**clicking here**</u>](https://discord.gg/zVDvY3nXKA).
:::

To link your account, you can run: `/discord_setup_account <discord username>`

This will send you a DM notification on Discord, where you can press the 'Connect' button to link your account!
