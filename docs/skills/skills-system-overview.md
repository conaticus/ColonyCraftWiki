# Skills Overview

In Colony Craft, you can develop your own skills to be a unique asset to other players in the server. These present various perks that will be useful for your progression. Each player can have one primary skill and a  one specialisation after mastering that skill.

### Primary Skill
To select a primary skill, use the `/skill` command, and you will be presented with the menu as seen below. You can only select **one** primary skill.

<img src="/img/primaryskill.png" width="30%" />

<br />
<br />

You can hover over these to see their different perks, or alternatively use the `/skill_perks [skill]` command. You can click one to make it your primary skill!

### Skill Specialisation
After reaching Level 5 you will have mastered your primary skill. At this point you can run the `/skill specialisation` command and specialise in one area of your skill, making you uniquely useful in the server.


<img src="/img/skillspecialisation.png" width="30%" />

### Summary of Your Skills

To check what skills you have at any time, use the `/skills` command. If you are in a colony, you can also use `/skills <playername>` to check the skills of another player in your colony.
