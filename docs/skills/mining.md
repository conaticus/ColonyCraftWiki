# Mining

The mining skill can be progressed by mining ores underground. By default, all ores have a drop chance of 25%. As you progress these rates will increase and you will eventually be able to get multiple drops from mining ores.

## Levels

**Level 1**
- +50% Iron and Coal Drop Chance (75%)

**Level 2**
- +35% Redstone, Lapis and Quartz Drop Chance (50%)
- Craft and Use Diamond Pickaxe

**Level 3**
- Craft Gas Mask
- Craft and Use Netherite Pickaxe
- +25% Diamond, Gold and Amethyst Drop Chance (50%)
- Unlock Efficiency Enchant
- 1.5x XP from ores

**Level 4**
- Craft and Use Special Sponge
- Unlock Silk Touch Enchant
- +35% Emerald and Diamond Drop Chance (50%)
- Quartz Block Always Drops
- 2x XP from ores

## Specialisations
### Fortifier
**Description:** Specialises in base defenses, can mine strong ores like Obsidian and Valtronium.  
**Perks:**
- Craft Strong Protection Block with 8 Acanthite and 1 Amethyst
- Can Mine Obsidian
- Can Mine Valtronium
- 100% Amethyst Drop Rate
- 25% Chance of 2x Amethyst Drops

### Deep Forger
**Description:** Best at extracting the rarest ores, higher drop rates all round.  
**Perks:**
- 100% Diamond and Netherite Drop Rate
- 15% Chance of 2x Drops on All Ores

### Industrial Miner
**Description:** Boosts drops for industrial ores such as coal and iron, can use stone cutters.  
**Perks:**
- 50% Chance 2x Drops on Iron, Coal, Gold
- 25% Chance 3x Drops on Iron, Coal, Gold
- Craft and Use Stone Cutter
