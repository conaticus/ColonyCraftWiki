# Combat

The combat skill can be progressed by killing mobs. You unlock various tools and perks as you progress (see [Netherite Recipes](/docs/recipes/netherite-tools-armour.md))

## Bones and Gunpowder

Bones and gunpowder can **only** be obtained by players with the Combat skill (starting from Level 0). These can be dropped by Skeletons. This is beneficial for farmers who might want to craft this into bonemeal, or those who wish to craft fireworks from gunpowder.

In Colony Craft, skeletons start with a 1/8 chance of dropping 1 bone and 1 gunpowder. If you kill a skeleton with an axe, there is a 1/4 chance it will drop bones. If you kill a skeleton with a bow, it has a 1/2 chance of dropping bones.

## Levels

**Level 1**
- Craft and Use Bows and Crossbows

**Level 2**
- Craft and Use Netherite Sword in Combat  
- Use Netherite Axe in Combat

**Level 3**
- 1.5x XP from Mobs

**Level 4**
- Craft and Equip Netherite Armour  
- 2x XP from Mobs

## Specialisations
### Knight
**Description:** Close-combat specialist with melee benefits and extra animal drops.  
**Perks:**
- Unlocks sharpness enchant
- Each melee hit has a 10% chance of doing 2x damage
- 50% chance of 2x drops when killing animals with sword or axe

### Archer
**Description:** Ranged specialist with enhanced bow abilities, can weaken enemies.  
**Perks:**
- Unlocks power and punch enchants
- Craft and Shoot Arrow of Weakness
- 50% chance of 2x drops when killing animals with a bow

### Medic
**Description:** Support combat role with healing and protection benefits, can slow enemies.  
**Perks:**
- Unlocks protection enchants
- Unlocks knockback enchantments
- Craft and use a splash potion of regeneration
- Craft and use a splash potion of slowness
- Craft and consume golden apple

### Brutalist
**Description:** Excels with axes, can weaken enemy defenses during combat.  
**Perks:**
- Axes have 3% per swing chance of destroying opponents shield
- Axes have 1% chance per swing of destroying a piece of opponents armour
- Axe does extra damage to a shield’s durability
- Axe does extra damage to armour’s durability

## XP Bonuses

If you kill a mob with any Axe or Bow, you will get an XP bonus.

Axe: 1.5x Bonus \
Bow: 2.5x Bonus
